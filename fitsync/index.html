FitSync privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Applies to FitSync for Android, version 2.1.0 and later.

Publisher: Tushar Bhatia. Contact: tusharbhatia0905@gmail.com.

Summary

  • FitSync keeps your data on your phone. There is no FitSync account, no FitSync server, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service.
  • FitSync only reads from Health Connect. It never writes to Health Connect and never changes or deletes records there.
  • FitSync does not sell, rent or share your health data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • The only time FitSync contacts the internet is the optional online food search, which is off until you turn it on. It sends only the words you search for.
  • You can delete everything FitSync stored with Profile > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

1. Data FitSync reads from Health Connect

Health Connect is Android's on-device store for health and fitness data. Other apps, such as Samsung Health, Google Fit or a watch's companion app, write records into it. FitSync asks for permission to read the types below. Every type is optional: you choose which ones to allow in Health Connect's own permission screen, and FitSync keeps working with only some or none of them.

Data typeWhat FitSync does with it
StepsShows your daily steps on Today, and step trends in Insights and Health history.
Active caloriesShows energy burned through activity on Today and in Insights, next to the calories you eat.
Total calories burnedShows total daily energy burned, including resting energy, on Today and in Insights.
DistanceShows the distance you covered each day on Today and in Insights.
Exercise sessionsShows workouts recorded by your watch or other apps on your Today timeline.
Heart rateShows daily average, lowest and highest heart rate on Today and in Insights.
Resting heart rateShows your resting heart rate on Today and its trend in Insights.
SleepShows sleep duration and sleep stages on Today and in Insights when your device records them.
WeightShows your latest weight in Profile and your weight trend in Insights.
HeightShows your height in Profile and uses it with your weight to work out BMI.
HydrationShown in Health history as water from other apps, separately from the water you log in FitSync. It is never added to your FitSync water total.
Body fatShows your latest body fat reading, its date and source app, and the change over the period, in the Body section of Insights.

With each record FitSync also keeps the information Health Connect attaches to it: the app that wrote it (for example "Samsung Health"), the device make, model and type when the writing app provides them, how it was recorded (automatically or entered by hand), and its time and time-zone offset. FitSync uses this to show where each value came from, to avoid counting the same activity twice when two apps record it, and to put records on the right day.

FitSync keeps daily heart-rate summaries, not individual heart-rate samples. It does not read GPS routes, nutrition, medical records, blood pressure, blood glucose or any type not listed above.

Two optional permissions

  • Older history. By default Health Connect lets an app read data from 30 days before the app was first given access. If you also allow access to older history, FitSync imports older records once and then keeps them up to date. If you do not, FitSync works with the default 30 days. You can allow it later from Profile > Health Connect > Devices & data sources. If you reinstall FitSync, Health Connect starts counting the 30 days again from the new connection.
  • Background reading. Off by default. If you turn on "Sync in background" and allow it, FitSync reads the same types about every 6 hours while the app is closed, so your data is current when you open it, and continues an older-history import a little at a time. Turning the switch off, or removing the permission, stops it.

3. Data FitSync creates to run

  • Sync state: which types have been imported, how far back, change tokens Health Connect issues, and the time of the first connection. These are used to import only what is new instead of re-reading everything.
  • A sync log with times, counts and error type names (no health values), shown in Profile > Health Connect > Diagnostics.
  • Which source apps you have hidden or ranked.

FitSync does not create advertising identifiers, profiles for marketing, or any data about you on other services.

4. How FitSync uses data

Only for features you can see in the app: dashboards, daily summaries, insights and trends you open, Health history, source attribution, reminders you turned on, and the export you request. FitSync does not use Health Connect data for advertising, for selling, for credit or insurance decisions, for profiling unrelated to these features, or to train models. FitSync's insights are simple summaries (totals, averages and changes over time); FitSync is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice.

FitSync's use of Health Connect data complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing and the internet

  • Health data is never sent off your phone by FitSync. FitSync has no server and includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • Online food search (optional, off by default). When you turn on "Search Open Food Facts" in Profile and search for a food, FitSync sends the words you typed to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) over an encrypted HTTPS connection to get food nutrition values. Nothing else from FitSync is included: no health data, food log, account or device identifier. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your phone's IP address and a generic app name with the request; its own privacy policy applies to what it receives. Turning the switch off stops all such requests.
  • Export (only when you ask). Profile > Export my data writes a JSON file of your FitSync data, including the copy of Health Connect data FitSync holds, to a place you choose with Android's file picker. Once saved, that file is outside FitSync's control: anyone or any app you give it to can read it. Store or share it with care.
  • Other apps. FitSync does not share data with other apps. It never writes to Health Connect, so nothing FitSync stores reaches apps that read Health Connect.
  • Selling. FitSync does not sell any data.
  • FitSync may disclose information only if the law requires it, and it holds no information off your phone that it could disclose.

6. Where data is stored and how long it is kept

All FitSync data is stored in FitSync's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Data you entered is kept until you delete it or delete all data.
  • Health Connect data is a copy that FitSync keeps up to date: when a record is changed or deleted in Health Connect, FitSync updates or removes its copy at the next sync. For daily totals this happens for the last 45 days at every sync, for older days at a monthly check, or at once with "Re-check history".
  • If you stop sharing a data type in Health Connect, FitSync deletes its copy of that type at the next sync. If you remove all of FitSync's Health Connect permissions, FitSync deletes all its copied Health Connect data.
  • If a source app stops writing to Health Connect, FitSync keeps the records it already copied and marks that source as having no recent records.
  • Android backup is turned off for FitSync. Your data is not included in Google cloud backups or device-to-device transfer, so a new phone or a reinstall starts empty. Use Export if you want your own copy.

7. Deleting your data

  • Single items: delete a food entry, water entry, workout, set, custom food or meal where it appears in the app.
  • One source app: Profile > Health Connect > Devices & data sources > the app > Delete FitSync's copy. This deletes only FitSync's copy; the records stay in Health Connect.
  • Disconnect Health Connect: Profile > Health Connect > Disconnect removes FitSync's access and deletes all copied Health Connect data.
  • Everything: Profile > Delete all data erases everything FitSync stored on the phone, cancels reminders and background sync, and then removes FitSync's Health Connect access.
  • Uninstalling FitSync deletes all of its data from the phone.
  • What FitSync cannot delete: records held by Health Connect or by the apps that wrote them (manage those in Health Connect or in the source app), and export files you saved elsewhere.

Because FitSync has no account and no server, there is no data held by the developer to request or delete.

8. Security

  • Data is kept in FitSync's private app storage, protected by Android's sandbox and your phone's lock screen and storage encryption. FitSync does not add its own database encryption.
  • FitSync can read health data only through the Health Connect permissions you grant, and it requests only read permissions.
  • The only network connection (optional food search) uses HTTPS; unencrypted connections are blocked by the app's network security settings.
  • FitSync does not write health values to the system log. Its error messages record only the type of error.
  • Android backup and device transfer are disabled for FitSync's data.

9. Children

FitSync is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. All data stays on the device.

10. Your choices

You can use FitSync without Health Connect, without any optional permission and without the internet. You can review or change FitSync's Health Connect permissions at any time in Health Connect > App permissions > FitSync, or from Profile > Health Connect in FitSync.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new "Last updated" date, and the in-app copy will be updated in the next version of the app. If FitSync ever starts sending data off your phone in a new way, it will ask you first.
