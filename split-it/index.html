Split-It Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026. Publisher: Tushar Bhatia. Contact: tusharbhatia0905@gmail.com.

Summary

Split-It keeps your expenses, groups, bills and UPI IDs on your phone only. The developer has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising SDKs, and receives none of your data.

Data the app stores (on your device only)

Expenses, groups and members' names, balances, bills and reminder schedules, optional UPI IDs (yours and your friends'), settings. Stored in the app's private storage; app backup (allowBackup) is disabled, so Android does not copy it to Google Drive. You can export your data from the app and delete it at any time by deleting entries or uninstalling the app (uninstall removes all of it).

Data that leaves your phone, only when you choose

  • Sharing/reminders: when you tap WhatsApp, email or Share, the text you see (names, amounts, optionally your UPI ID and a pay link) is handed to the app you pick. That app's privacy policy then applies.
  • Pay by UPI: opens your UPI app with the payee's UPI ID, name and exact amount. Split-It never processes or moves money and does not see whether you paid.
  • Imports/exports: files go only where you save or pick them.

Google Play and subscriptions

Split-It Plus is a subscription sold by Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; Split-It never sees your card or UPI payment credentials. The Google Play Billing library (a Google component) communicates with Google Play to show prices and check whether your Google account has an active subscription; Google's privacy policy applies to that. Split-It stores only a local cached copy of "Plus active / not active" and its check time. The app's merged manifest includes the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions because the Billing library declares them; Split-It's own code makes no network requests.

Notifications

Bill reminders are local notifications created on your device. Notification text (bill name, amount) may appear on your lock screen according to your Android settings.

Children

Split-It is not directed at children under 13.
