ReelForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026. Publisher: Tushar Bhatia. Contact: tusharbhatia0905@gmail.com.

Summary

ReelForge is a video-editing app that works entirely on your device. It does not have user accounts, does not contain advertising, does not include analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not have permission to access the internet. The developer does not receive any of your photos, videos, music or personal information. If a version offers a Premium subscription, the purchase is handled by Google Play (see "Purchases"); the developer does not run a server and does not receive your payment or purchase data.

What the app accesses

  • Photos and videos you choose. You select them with the Android system photo picker (or the system file picker on older Android versions). ReelForge can read only the items you select, and only to build your reel. Temporary processed copies (for example scaled photos) are stored in the app's private cache while a reel is being made and are deleted when the render ends, is cancelled or fails (and any leftovers are removed the next time you make a reel).
  • Music you choose (optional). If you pick your own audio file, ReelForge reads it through the system file picker for that render only.
  • Your finished reels. Reels are stored in the app's private storage ("My creations"). If you tap Save (or enable "Save to gallery automatically") a copy is written to Movies/ReelForge on your device. If you tap Share, the reel is handed to the app you choose in the Android share sheet; what that app does with it is governed by that app's own policy.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+). ReelForge shows a notification with the progress of a render and when it is finished. You can decline the permission; reels are still made.
  • Template favourites and recently used templates. The ids of templates you favourite or use are stored locally on your device so they can be shown again. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Purchases (only when Premium is offered). Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. ReelForge never sees your card or payment details. Google Play tells the app, on your device, which subscription your Google account holds (product, state, renewal status and a purchase token, with a signature). The app checks the signature and stores the last verified confirmation locally so Premium keeps working offline for up to 72 hours. This information stays on your device; it is not sent to the developer. Google's own privacy policy governs what Google processes (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Cancel or manage the subscription in Google Play.
  • Settings (theme, "save automatically", export quality) are stored locally on your device.

What the app does not do

No account or sign-in. No advertising, no advertising ID. No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no third-party SDKs that collect data. No location, contacts, microphone, camera or phone-identifier access. No network connection of its own (the app does not hold the INTERNET permission); communication with Google Play for purchases is done by the Google Play services/Play Store apps on your device, not by ReelForge. No server operated by the developer.

Data collection, sharing and retention

The developer collects no personal data and shares no data with third parties. Everything the app keeps (reels, thumbnails, settings, favourites/recent templates, the last verified purchase confirmation) stays on your device until you delete it: delete a reel inside the app, use Settings > Clear temporary files, delete the copies in your gallery yourself, or uninstall the app (which removes the app's private data).

Children

ReelForge is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone.

Backup and device transfer

ReelForge excludes everything it stores (reels, settings, favourites and the cached purchase confirmation) from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer (allowBackup=false plus data-extraction rules). After moving to a new phone the app starts empty; Premium (if offered) is restored from your Google account with "Restore purchases", and reels are not copied.

Security

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server-side copy to protect. Shared reels are exposed to other apps only through a read-only, per-share permission grant for a single file.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes (for example if a future version adds a backend, online template downloads or analytics) the updated policy will be published at the same address and the app's Data safety form will be updated before such a version is released. Questions: tusharbhatia0905@gmail.com.
