BlueBoard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026. Publisher: Tushar Bhatia. Contact: tusharbhatia0905@gmail.com.

Summary

BlueBoard shows the Bluetooth and local-network devices around you. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server. The developer receives none of your data. Nothing leaves your phone or your home network.

What BlueBoard uses, and why

  • Nearby devices permission (Bluetooth scan and connect). Used to list connected, paired and nearby Bluetooth devices and to read a battery level when a device reports one. BlueBoard declares that it never derives your location from Bluetooth scans, and it does not request any location permission.
  • Local network. Used to discover smart-home devices on your own Wi-Fi (mDNS and UPnP). BlueBoard contacts only private network addresses, never the internet. Android requires the INTERNET permission for this local access. Discovery can be switched off in Settings.
  • An audio file you choose (optional). If you use the player, BlueBoard reads the single file you picked through the system file picker and plays it through the output Android selects. A media-playback service keeps it playing in the background.
  • Installed companion apps. BlueBoard asks Android whether certain official companion apps are installed, so it can open them for you.

What is stored on your phone

  • Devices you have paired or starred.
  • Connection history (device name and address, connect or disconnect, time), kept up to 90 days and up to 200 entries per device. You can switch it off and clear it in Settings.
  • Playback groups (device names and addresses) and your settings.

Names and addresses of other nearby devices are kept in memory only. Stored data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Wi-Fi passwords and Bluetooth keys are never read or stored.

Sharing

BlueBoard collects no data for the developer and shares no data with third parties.

Deletion

Clear the connection history in Settings, or uninstall BlueBoard to remove everything it stored.

Children

BlueBoard is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Material changes will be published at the same address and described in the release notes.
